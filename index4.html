The Process

From File to Finished Piece

Simple, transparent, and built around your timeline with end-to-end status tracking.

1
Step 01

You send the design

Upload your STL, STEP, or OBJ file, or simply send photos, hand sketches, and target dimensions.

2
Step 02

We check & prep

We inspect wall thickness, orientation, support placement, and optimize slicing parameters for strength.

3
Step 03

We print carefully

Calibrated beds, tuned temperatures, and layer-by-layer monitoring in our climate-controlled lab.

4
Step 04

Clean, check & pack

Support removal, tolerance verification with digital calipers, deburring, and protective cushioning.

5
Step 05

Fast ship across India

Secure bubble-wrapped packaging with express door-to-door courier tracking across all Indian pincodes.
